radar-signal-processing-sim

Pulse-Doppler radar signal processing simulator in C++17: LFM pulse compression, MTI, Doppler FFT, 2-D CA/OS-CFAR, tracking and Monte Carlo Pd/Pfa analysis.

Viewer: X-band surveillance

Four targets over ground clutter, three-pulse MTI, CA-CFAR, 8 CPIs with tracks.

Viewer: formation, OS-CFAR

Two aircraft 105 m apart; OS-CFAR detects the wingman that CA-CFAR masks.

Viewer: S-band benchmark

60 km window, 128 pulses x 5285 samples per CPI, Taylor tapers.

Doxygen documentation of the library headers and sources.
